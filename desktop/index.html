FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Straight answers. Still unsure? Call 1300 737 562.

Will our data be used to train public AI models?

Your AI tools are set up through governed access. Sensitive data is removed by the GenAI firewall before it reaches a model, and we configure each AI provider’s data settings as part of your governance setup.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude, plus governed access to 50–500+ models through the AIdome platform.

Do we need to replace our systems?

No. Agents connect to what you already use, such as Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Xero, MYOB and your CRM.

Our staff are worried AI will replace them. How do you handle that?

Openly. Change management is the biggest part of our program: we involve staff early, focus AI on busywork, and build AI Champions in every team.

How long does it take?

A typical program runs for about 12 weeks. Training starts early, and no agent goes live before your people are trained.

How much does it cost?

Packages start from A$1,500 (Foundation), A$2,500 (Growth) and A$5,500 (Enterprise). The AI security platform is from A$35.00 per user per month, plus from A$550 per user once off. All prices exclude GST. Payment plans are available through grenke.

How do you help with the new Privacy Act rules?

We align your AI policy and records to the Privacy Act, including the automated decision transparency rules that start on 10 December 2026, as well as Essential Eight and ISO/IEC 42001 guidance.
