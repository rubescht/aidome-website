The change journey

Five steps from “why AI?” to “how did we work without it?”

Aligned to the widely used ADKAR change model.

  1. A

    Awareness

    Why AI, why now?
    • Leadership briefing
    • Clear AI vision for staff
    • Risks and rules explained
  2. D

    Desire

    What’s in it for me?
    • Address job fears openly
    • AI Champions in every team
    • Quick wins shown early
  3. K

    Knowledge

    How do I use it?
    • Role-based training
    • Safe & secure AI for all
    • Your AI policy, in plain English
  4. A

    Ability

    Can I do it on my own work?
    • Hands-on labs with your tasks
    • Prompt library per role
    • Monthly drop-in help
  5. R

    Reinforcement

    Will it stick?
    • Adoption dashboard
    • Recognise and share wins
    • Refresh as AI changes
